Mirror League · Mobile shell bug

The “Sign in” button hides under the browser chrome

On mobile, the side nav is an off-canvas drawer whose Sign in chip is pinned to the bottom with margin-top:auto. Because the drawer is sized with 100vh and doesn’t scroll, its bottom edge sits underneath the browser’s bottom toolbar — so the one button a logged-out visitor needs is clipped and, on short screens, unreachable.

Why it happens — three compounding causes

All three live in the one @media (max-width:760px) .side block.

1

Static viewport unit

The drawer uses height:100vh. On mobile, vh is the largest viewport (toolbar collapsed), so the bottom overshoots the visible area when the toolbar is shown. The .app wrapper already switched to 100dvh — the drawer didn’t.

2

No scroll fallback

The drawer is a flex column with no overflow-y. When Global + League nav + switcher + chip exceed the screen, the bottom content is clipped with no way to reach it.

3

No safe-area inset

Bottom padding is a flat 14px. On notched phones the home indicator eats it, nudging the chip further out of reach. Needs env(safe-area-inset-bottom).
